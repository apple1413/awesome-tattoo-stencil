## Traditional Stencil Methods

Before AI tools, artists relied on manual tracing and dedicated hardware. These methods still work and are worth understanding:

- **Hand tracing with hectograph (spirit) paper** — Trace your design onto stencil paper with a ballpoint pen. Time-consuming for complex portraits but zero cost. See the [tattoo stencil paper guide](https://tattoostencil.org/blog/tattoo-stencil-paper-guide) for paper types.
- **Thermal copier / thermal printer** — Machines like the classic Thermofax burn the design onto thermal stencil paper. Fast and accurate, but the hardware is a real investment. The [tattoo stencil printer guide](https://tattoostencil.org/blog/tattoo-stencil-printer-guide) breaks down the options — and free alternatives like an [AI tattoo stencil tool](https://tattoostencil.org) that prints on plain paper.
- **Freehand directly on skin** — Some artists skip stencils entirely for organic work. Requires strong fundamentals; not recommended for beginners or portrait work.
